Designing loyalty cards for a car wash IoT service — from idea to launch

CloudCore is an IoT platform for self-service car washes. I took the loyalty-card feature from the first idea to launch: owners order branded RFID cards, activate them right at the wash terminal and run the whole programme — rules, balances, analytics — from the app.
My contribution: research with owners and customers, the product concept, end-to-end UI design and prototyping.

Role
Product / UX / UI Designer
Timeline

Methods

PROBLEM

Car wash owners have no real way to retain customers — there’s simply no loyalty tool

The self-service car wash market is growing, and competition is getting tougher. Owners had no way to encourage repeat visits or stand out from competitors. At the same time, as a platform, we wanted to increase our value for B2B clients — without taking on the logistics of physical card production.

“I need a way to make customers come back to me — not go to the place next door.”

Timur
Car wash owner (B2B)

“I want to see how much I’ve accumulated — and get something in return for staying loyal.”

Alex
Car wash customer (B2C)
RESEARCH

The audience favors physical cards over convenience — they shape the format

Competitor analysis and interviews with car wash owners revealed a clear pattern: while digital cards in Wallet are convenient, they don’t work for the diverse audience of self-service car washes. Customers vary widely in age and digital literacy, making physical branded cards a must-have. At the same time, none of the competitors offered a built-in loyalty solution at the IoT platform level — which opened up a clear opportunity for us.

IDEATION

Exploring card integration: three approaches, one final solution

The toughest part of the project was card onboarding. Since we don’t produce the cards ourselves, we had no way to verify their data using traditional methods.

Rejected solutions
Hypothesis 1

Manual card number entryToo time-consuming at scale. Spreadsheet import was a partial workaround — but it didn’t scale well and wasn’t convenient for most owners.

SOLUTION

Loyalty cards as part of the platform — without handling logistics on our sideCar wash owners order branded RFID cards from third-party vendors and activate them directly through the wash terminal. In the app, they set up loyalty rules, manage customer balances, and track card usage through analytics. We provide the software layer only — full responsibility for the cards stays with the owner, removing operational risks on our side.

Terminal-based RFID reader + card onboarding mode, with built-in promotionsWe used the built-in RFID reader in the terminal board. The owner enables a dedicated mode in the app and taps the cards on the terminal — they are automatically added to the system. The instructions are embedded directly in the interface.

RESULTS
The feature has gone live and is now part of car wash owners’ daily operations.
Car wash owners now have a dedicated tool to keep customers coming back.
This feature helped the platform stand out in a competitive market.
LEARNINGS

Business context over design intuitionBefore jumping into design, it’s important to define the boundaries between the product and the client. The card solution only came together after a deep understanding of the business model.

Hardware defines the UXPhysical constraints like RFID and terminal capabilities pushed us toward an elegant solution — leveraging existing infrastructure rather than creating something new.

Balancing two different mindsetsB2B and B2C users think about the product in fundamentally different ways. Working solo, without a design team, meant constantly switching context and validating decisions on my own.
